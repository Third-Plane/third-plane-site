# Knight's Dilemma animation storyboard

The visual system uses only three elements: a knight silhouette, a pawn
silhouette and board geometry. Pawns carry the meaning from section to section;
squares, files and ranks explain how their organization changes. The animation
sits in reserved rails above cards or beside dark shells, never behind content.

| Stop | Settled composition | Motion inside the section | Story |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | One large knight on the three-plane mark | The knight rises into position | Individual execution begins as the hero |
| Why now | Six armed pawns on a top rail facing the exhibit knight | The rank marches toward the knight | Organization defeats individual mastery |
| Org chart | Four identical pawns, each contained by a different board boundary | The pawns resolve into the four card positions | Labor requires role, authority, supervision and record |
| Chokepoint | Three pawn files with a visibly taller coral center queue | The file spacing compresses | Placement is the largest operating constraint |
| Solutions | A three-pawn formation and one pawn entering an existing formation | The first formation spreads; the incoming pawn joins the second | Workforce redraws the board; Inbox enters the board already in use |
| Worked example | Three empty squares joined by a rank line | One teal pawn advances across all three squares and turns coral at the decision | One flow is carried end to end |
| Deployments | Six occupied squares in a two-by-three board | One completed move expands into six positions | A proven move becomes a repeatable deployment pattern |
| Alpine | Three occupied squares on one rank | The three positions settle into alignment | Execution, authority and proof share a platform but remain distinct |
| Integrations | Three pawns just inside the edge of the dark system board | The pieces move inward from the boundary | Existing systems become entry points, not replacement targets |
| Evidence | Three occupied provenance squares | The positions spread from one center into a record | Results resolve into origin, independence and isolation |
| Contact | The three-plane mark | The bars extend to full width | The system becomes the final identity |

## Transition grammar

1. Normal scrolling is preserved; there is no scroll snapping to skip over the
   animation.
2. Every scene moves with its section. Outgoing pieces leave with outgoing
   content and incoming pieces rise with incoming content.
3. Pawn-to-pawn transitions follow file-then-rank paths at the section seam.
4. The knight and final brand mark use a clean crossfade because they are not
   pawns.
5. Forms stay above the page surface at high contrast, inside measured gaps or
   inside the dark integration shell.

## Constraints

- No generic business icons, miniature documents or unrelated glyphs.
- No low-opacity ornament hidden behind cards.
- No movement through headings or body copy.
- Canvas remains pointer-transparent and decorative.
- Disabled below 901px and under reduced motion.
